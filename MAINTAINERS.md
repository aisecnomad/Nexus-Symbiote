## Responsibilities

The lead maintainer owns pull-request reviews and merges, issue and security-report triage, PyPI/TestPyPI and container releases, CI and branch-protection maintenance, dependency lock regeneration, and roadmap direction. Live status of repository and release controls is tracked in [repository controls](docs/repository-controls.md#operator-prerequisites).

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who demonstrate sustained, high-quality participation — strong grasp of the [security model](docs/security-model.md), careful supply-chain hygiene, thorough reviews, and responsive community engagement — may be invited to join as maintainers. Start with [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and open issues.
